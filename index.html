Why I'm running

Sports is more than winning matches.

For me, sports has taught discipline, teamwork, consistency and leadership, and how to handle both success and failure. I want every NIAT student to get those same opportunities.

NIAT has a lot of sporting potential. What students need are the right opportunities, facilities, guidance and exposure. I want sports to be a regular part of student life, where people practise consistently, compete with confidence and proudly represent NIAT.

I believe in taking action instead of waiting for someone else to solve the problem.

My vision

Participate. Improve. Compete. Represent.

A sports culture at NIAT where every student gets a genuine opportunity. Here is how I'll build it.

1
Equipment and facilities first
Students can't perform without the basics. I'll identify what each sport needs and work to make that equipment available.
2
Official NIAT Pune teams
Teams across different sports, with regular practice schedules, transparent trials and better communication, so we prepare all year and not only when a tournament is announced.
3
Inter-NIAT Sports Event
An internal competition inspired by the inter-hostel and inter-group spirit at institutes like the IITs. A place to discover your ability, build team spirit, and where even beginners can join in.
4
More women in sport
Keep building an environment where girls feel equally encouraged and supported in every sport, continuing what I've already started on the Sports Council.
5
Exposure beyond campus
More participation in external inter-college tournaments, with the long-term goal of making NIAT Pune known for its sporting achievements.
